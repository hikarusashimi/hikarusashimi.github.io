Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

What this app is

chaos-to-chord is a personal tool used only by its developer to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

With the user's permission (Google OAuth), the app uploads video files, titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and sets the publish time of those videos. It does not read, collect or store any other Google user data.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the developer's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's, and no data is sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
